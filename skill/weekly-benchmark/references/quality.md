# Report quality bar

Check before publishing. Fail any item, go back and fix it.

## Structure

1. **Opening: at most three things.** The three that demand action or change a bet. Everything else goes to the appendix.
2. Each of the three: **what changed → source → why it matters for this product → what to do about it**. That order, in 3–5 lines.
3. Appendix: everything else that changed, as a list.
4. Footer: what was checked, what failed to collect, how many findings the critic cut.

## Tests

- **Link test:** does every factual claim carry a clickable URL? If not, cut it.
- **So-what test:** read each item and ask "so what?". If the answer doesn't connect to the decision declared in the config, it's appendix, not opening.
- **Last-week test:** swap the date and does the text still read the same? Then it's description, not benchmark. Cut it.
- **Pattern test:** has this happened before? Check `benchmark/history.md`. "Third week running" is worth ten times "this week".
- **Length test:** does it fit on one screen? Nobody reads eight pages on a Monday morning.

## Tone

Direct and specific. Names, numbers, dates. No "it's important to note that", no "in the current landscape", no decorative hedging. If confidence is low, say the confidence — don't dilute the sentence.
